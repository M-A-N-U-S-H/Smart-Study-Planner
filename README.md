*# 📚 Smart Study Planner*



*A smart study planning application that helps students organize their preparation based on subjects, exam dates, and available study hours.*



*## 🎯 Problem Statement*



*Students often find it difficult to distribute their available study time effectively when multiple exams are approaching.*



*The Smart Study Planner solves this problem by generating a day-by-day study timetable based on:*



*- Subjects*

*- Exam dates*

*- Available study hours per day*

*- Exam urgency*



*## 💡 Solution*



*The student enters their details, subjects, exam dates, and available study hours through a Streamlit interface.*



*The application then:*



*1. Calculates the number of days remaining until each exam.*

*2. Determines the urgency of each subject.*

*3. Gives higher priority to subjects with closer exams.*

*4. Distributes the available study hours.*

*5. Generates a day-by-day study timetable.*

*6. Provides a progress checklist for tracking completed subjects.*



*## ✨ Features*



*- 👨‍🎓 Student details input*

*- 📚 Multiple subject support*

*- 📅 Exam date input*

*- ⏰ Available study hours per day*

*- 📊 Automatic timetable generation*

*- 🚨 Exam urgency calculation*

*- 📋 Progress checklist*

*- ⚠️ Empty subject validation*

*- 🖥️ Interactive Streamlit interface*
